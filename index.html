1. Chest Drain Insertion

[Diagram: Triangle of Safety]

Indication: Traumatic haemothorax/pneumothorax with respiratory distress OR Tension pneumothorax if needle failed.

Equipment Required [Location: HALO Trolley Drawer 1]

  • 28-32Fr chest drain
  • Surgical prep (Chlorhexidine)
  • 1% Lidocaine 20mL
  • Scalpel (size 10)
  • Spencer Wells forceps
  • Mattress suture (1-0 silk)
  • Sterile gloves & Gown
  • Underwater seal (-20cmH₂O)

1. Call & Assess

Inform Consultant & NIC.
Request Surgery if massive haemothorax anticipated.
Confirm Indication: USS/CXR if stable.

2. Preparation

Consent: Explain risks (pain, bleeding, infection).
Position: 30-45° sitting, arm abducted 90°.
Landmark: Triangle of Safety (5th ICS, Ant. to Mid-axillary).

3. Procedure

Clean & Drape: Strict asepsis.
Anaesthetise: Infiltrate skin to pleura (1% Lidocaine).
Incision: 2-3cm transverse, SUPERIOR to rib.
Dissect: Blunt dissection with forceps to pleura.
Sweep: Insert finger. Confirm space. Sweep adhesions.
CRITICAL: NEVER USE A TROCAR
Insert: Guide drain posterior & superior.

4. Completion

Connect: Underwater seal. Confirm swing/bubble.
Secure: Mattress suture (1-0 Silk). Dress.
CXR: Urgent check. Monitor output hourly.
Surgical Review: >1500mL initial OR >200mL/hr (2-4 hrs)

2. Emergency Thoracotomy

Indication: Traumatic arrest. Penetrating < 10 mins OR Blunt < 5 mins.

Equipment Required [Location: HALO Trolley Top Shelf]

  • Scalpel (Size 10 & 20)
  • Rib spreaders (Finochietto)
  • Heavy scissors (Mayo)
  • Gigli saw
  • Toothed forceps (Bonney's)
  • Large suction (Yankauer x2)
  • Aortic Clamp
  • 3-0 Prolene / Skin Stapler
  • Internal Paddles (or Ext. Pads)

1. Activation

Call Consultant & NIC.
Call Cardiothoracic Reg IMMEDIATELY.
Activate Massive Haemorrhage (Pack A).
Contraindication: Asystole > 10 mins.

2. Preparation

Continue CPR (unless interfering).
Intubate & Ventilate 100% O2.
Rapid prep (Splash Chlorhexidine).
Right arm abducted 90°.
KNIFE TO SKIN - STOP & CHECK

3. Procedure: Left Anterolateral

Incision: 4th/5th ICS (Nipple level), Mid-axillary to sternum.
Cut: Single bold incision (Skin & Subcut).
Muscle: Stop vent. Cut intercostals with heavy scissors.
Retract: Insert spreaders (Handle DOWN). Crank open.

4. HOT Protocol

H: Haemorrhage: Twist lung hilum. Clamp aorta against spine.
O: Open Pericardium: Lift with forceps. Cut ant. to Phrenic.
T: Treat Heart: 'Birth' heart. Plug holes (Finger/Foley/Staple).
Massage: Two hands/flat palms if no output.
Clamshell: Extend across sternum if needed.
6
Emergency Thoracotomy
1. IMMEDIATE Call
  • Consultant + NIC
  • Cardiothoracic Reg URGENTLY
  • Major Haemorrhage Protocol
2. Preparation
  • Thoracotomy tray ready
  • Scalpel, spreaders, clamps
  • Blood warmer primed

3. Throughout
  • Clear team roles
  • Monitor for ROSC
  • Prepare for Theatre transfer

  • H: Haemorrhage (lung twist, aortic clamp)
  • O: Open pericardium (ant. to phrenic)
  • T: Treat cardiac wound / Internal Massage
↓

  • Immediate transfer to CT Surgery
  • Continue internal massage if needed
  • Massive transfusion ongoing
↓
Key Considerations
  • Clamshell: Extend across sternum if bilateral/poor access
  • Survival: Penetrating ~15%, Blunt <2%

3. Escharotomy

[Diagram: Incision Lines]

Indication: Circumferential full-thickness burns with compartment syndrome.

Equipment Required [Location: HALO Trolley Drawer 3]

  • Scalpel (Size 10 or 22)
  • Electrocautery (Preferred)
  • Sterile drapes & gloves
  • Marking pen
  • Dressings (Kaltostat)
  • Ketamine (Sedation)

1. Call for Help

Consultant + NIC + Burns Centre.
Alert Theatre (Fasciotomy risk).

2. Preparation

Position: Supine, limbs abducted.
Mark: Draw incision lines (Mid-axial).
Sedation: Ketamine (Full thickness is insensate, but edges hurt).
TIMING CRITICAL: PERFORM WITHIN 2 HOURS

3. Limb Procedure

Incise: BOTH Medial AND Lateral mid-axial lines.
Extend: Proximal to distal, crossing joints.
Depth: Through eschar until fat bulges. Avoid deep fascia.
Hands: Radial (Thumb) & Ulnar (Little finger). Avoid webs.

4. Chest Procedure

Vertical: Bilateral anterior axillary lines.
Transverse: Join at costal margin ('Roman Breastplate').
Goal: Allow chest excursion.

5. Post-Procedure

Apply haemostatic dressings. Elevate.
Reassess pulses/ventilation.
8
Escharotomy

  • Alert Theatre
2. Preparation
  • Position patient
  • Scalpel + Cautery
  • Dressings ready
  • No anaesthesia usually needed

  • WITHIN 2 HOURS

  • Reduced chest expansion

4. Landmark Subclavian Line

[Diagram: Clavicle Landmarks]

Indication: Central access needed; US unavailable/failed.

Equipment Required [Location: Line Trolley]

  • CVC Kit (Triple Lumen)
  • Sterile Gown, Gloves, Drape
  • Chlorhexidine
  • 1% Lidocaine (10mL)
  • 10mL Syringe
  • Guidewire, Dilator, Scalpel (11)
  • Suture & Tegaderm
  • Saline flushes

1. Call for Help

Consultant + NIC.
Request senior support (Anaesthetics/ITU) if inexperienced.

2. Preparation

Position: Supine, 15° Head down (Trendelenburg).
Head turned away. Towel roll between scapulae (optional).
Sterile prep. Flush all lumens.

3. Procedure

Landmark: Junction of middle/medial third clavicle.
Anaesthetise: Infiltrate 1-2cm inferior to clavicle.
Insert: Needle on 10mL syringe (bevel up).
Aim: Towards suprasternal notch, under clavicle.
Advance: Aspirate continuously.
Flashback: Confirm venous (dark/non-pulsatile).
Safety: Occlude hub (Air embolus risk).
Seldinger: Wire -> Incise -> Dilate -> Catheter.

4. Post-Procedure

Aspirate/Flush all lumens.
Secure and dress.
URGENT CXR: Check tip & pneumothorax.
10

5. Lateral Canthotomy

[Diagram: Canthus Anatomy]

Indication: Orbital Compartment Syndrome (IOP >40mmHg / Proptosis + Vision Loss).

Equipment Required [Location: Eye Trolley / HALO Box]

  • 1-2% Lidocaine/Adrenaline
  • Small syringe + 25G needle
  • Straight scissors (Iris)
  • Toothed forceps (Adson)
  • Haemostat (Mosquito)
  • Sterile gauze & saline
  • Irrigation fluid (if chemical)

1. Immediate Action

Call Consultant + NIC.
Call Ophthalmology (Do not delay).
Assess VA/IOP if possible.
TIME CRITICAL < 2 HOURS. DO NOT WAIT FOR CT.

2. Procedure

Prep: Clean & Inject LA into lateral canthus.
Crimp: Clamp canthus 1cm with haemostat (30-60s).
Canthotomy: Cut crimped area horizontally (~1cm).
Cantholysis: Retract lower lid. Locate inferior crus (guitar string).
Cut: Point scissors inferoposteriorly. Cut crus.
Check: Lid should be mobile.
Fail? Cut superior crus if inadequate.

3. Post-Procedure

Recheck IOP & Vision.
Apply antibiotic ointment. Light dressing.
Arrange CT Orbits.
12
Lateral Canthotomy

  • Ophthalmology URGENTLY
  • TIME CRITICAL: < 2 Hours
2. Diagnosis
  • IOP > 40mmHg OR
  • Proptosis + Tense Globe
  • RAPD + Vision Loss
3. Equipment
  • Small scissors
  • Toothed forceps
  • Local Anaesthetic
  • Haemostat
↓
Canthotomy + Cantholysis
  • 1. Cut full thickness lateral canthus (horizontal ~1cm)
  • 2. CANTHOLYSIS (ESSENTIAL): Open scissors vertically
  • 3. Cut inferior crus until lower lid freely mobile
  • 4. Superior cantholysis if inadequate
↓

  • CT Orbits
  • Urgent Ophthalmology review

6. Major Haemorrhage Control

Indication: Life-threatening haemorrhage.

Equipment Required [Location: Resus/Blood Bank]

  • Rapid Infuser (Belmont)
  • Fluid Warmer
  • MHP Pack A (Porter to Blood Bank)
  • Large IV (14G/16G) / IO
  • Pelvic Binder / Tourniquets
  • Celox Gauze
  • TXA (1g) + Calcium

1. Activation

Call 2222: "Activate Major Haemorrhage Protocol".
Location: ED Resus.
Alert Theatre / IR / Porter.
Assign Scribe.

2. Immediate Control

Pressure: Direct to bleed.
Limb: Tourniquet.
Pelvis: Binder (Greater trochanters).
Wound: Pack with haemostatic gauze.

3. Resuscitation

Access: 2x Large Bore IV or IO.
Labs: FBC, U&E, Coag, Fibrinogen, X-match.
TXA: 1g IV bolus (if <3h).
Products: Pack A (4 RBC + 4 FFP). Ratio 1:1:1.
Target SBP: 80-90mmHg (Radial pulse).
*Head Injury: Target SBP >110mmHg.

4. Monitoring

VBG q15mins.
Goals: Hb>70, pH>7.2, Temp>36, Ca>1.1.
Give Calcium (10mL 10%) per 4 units.
Prevent Hypothermia (Bair Hugger).
14
Major Haemorrhage Control
1. ACTIVATE PROTOCOL
  • Call Switchboard
  • State Location + Loss
  • Allocate roles
2. Immediate Control
  • Direct Pressure
  • Tourniquet (Limb)
  • Pelvic Binder
  • Haemostatic Packing
3. Resuscitation
  • TXA 1g IV (within 3h)
  • Permissive hypotension (80-90)
  • NO Crystalloid
  • Blood Products Only
↓
Blood Products 1:1:1 Ratio
  • Initial: 4 Units RBC + 4 Units FFP
  • Add 1 unit Platelets per 4 RBC
  • Warm all products
  • Use rapid transfuser
↓
Monitor & Adjust
  • VBG every 15-30 min
  • Target: Hb >70, pH >7.2, Ca >1.1
  • Cryoprecipitate if Fibrinogen < 1.5
  • Surgical/IR for definitive control

7. MaxFax Trauma

[Diagram: Nasal Packing]

Indication: Severe maxillofacial trauma with uncontrolled bleeding.

Equipment Required [Location: ENT/Airway Trolley]

  • Headlight
  • Suction x2 (Yankauer)
  • Magill forceps
  • Thudichum speculum
  • Rapid Rhino / Merocel
  • Foley Catheter (12-14Fr)
  • Umbilical clamp
  • TXA/Adrenaline soak

1. Call for Help

Consultant + NIC.
Anaesthetics (Difficult airway risk).
MaxFax / ENT URGENTLY.

2. Assessment

Position: Upright, lean forward (if C-spine allows).
Airway: High flow O2 + Suction.
Source: Anterior? Posterior? Oral?
Check for loose teeth/fragments.

3. Control

Oral: Direct pressure/suture.
Anterior: Suction. Insert Rapid Rhino. Inflate.
Posterior (if fails): Foley Catheter.
Insert Foley (12-14Fr) to nasopharynx.
Inflate balloon (10-15mL water).
Retract against choana.
Pack anteriorly (maintain traction).
Secure with umbilical clamp (padded).

4. Adjuncts

IV TXA 1g.
Correct coagulopathy.
DO NOT REMOVE POSTERIOR PACKS IN ED.
16
MaxFax Trauma
1. IMMEDIATE Call
  • Consultant + NIC
  • Anaesthetics
  • MaxFax / ENT Urgently
2. Airway & Position
  • Sit upright if conscious
  • Lean forward / Spit out blood
  • Continuous Suction
  • Prepare for Intubation
3. Identify Source
  • Anterior vs Posterior
  • Oropharyngeal
  • Facial Fractures
↓
Control Bleeding
  • Direct Pressure + Anterior Packing
  • POSTERIOR: Foley 14Fr to nasopharynx
  • Inflate 10-15ml, pull anteriorly
  • Pack anteriorly, secure clamp
↓
Adjuncts & Monitoring
  • TXA 1g if major bleeding
  • Large bore IV, Crossmatch
  • Don't remove posterior packs in ED
  • Theatre/IR for definitive control

8. Resuscitative Hysterotomy

Indication: Maternal arrest (≥20 weeks). No ROSC at 4 mins.

Equipment Required [Location: HALO Trolley / Obstetric Pack]

  • Scalpel (Size 10 & 20)
  • Heavy scissors (Mayo)
  • Cord Clamps x2
  • Abdominal swabs
  • Suction
  • Neonatal Resusitaire
  • Spencer Wells

1. Activation

Call Consultant + NIC.
Obstetric Reg IMMEDIATELY.
Neonatal Team.
Allocate Timekeeper.

2. Criteria

Gestation: Fundus ≥ Umbilicus.
Arrest: No ROSC at 4 mins.
PERFORM BY 5 MINUTES FROM ARREST

3. Preparation

Continue CPR + LUD (Left Uterine Displacement).
Splash Betadine.
KNIFE TO SKIN - STOP & CHECK

4. Procedure

Skin Incision: Vertical Midline (Xiphisternum to Pubis). Deep.
Uterus: Small vertical incision (lower segment). Lift wall.
Extend: Cut vertically with scissors (Guard fetus).
Deliver: Baby out. Clamp cord x2. Cut. Hand over.

5. Post-Delivery

Continue Maternal CPR.
Deliver placenta if separating.
Oxytocin 10u IV/IM. Pack uterus.
Transfer to Theatre.
18
Resuscitative Hysterotomy
1. IMMEDIATE Call
  • Consultant + NIC
  • Obstetric Registrar
  • Neonatal Team if ≥24 weeks
2. Indication
  • Pregnancy ≥20 weeks
  • Maternal Arrest
  • No ROSC after 4 min
  • GOAL: Delivery by 5 mins
3. During CPR
  • CPR + Left Uterine Displacement
  • NO sterile technique
  • Neonatal area ready
↓
Hysterotomy Technique
  • Vertical midline incision: Pubis to Umbilicus
  • Vertical uterine incision (extend with fingers)
  • Deliver baby, clamp/cut cord immediately
  • Hand to neonatal team
↓
Post-Delivery
  • Continue CPR (Relieves aortocaval compression)
  • Deliver Placenta / Pack Uterus
  • Oxytocin 10 units
  • Emergency Theatre for repair

9. Open Thoracostomy

Indication: Massive haemothorax / Tension Pneumothorax (Failed needle) / Traumatic Arrest.

Equipment Required [Location: HALO Trolley / Chest Drain Pack]

  • Scalpel (Size 10)
  • Spencer Wells / Kelly forceps
  • Chlorhexidine
  • Sterile gloves
  • Chest Drain (28-32Fr) - if ROSC

1. Call for Help

Consultant + NIC.
CT Surgery if massive.
Airway support.

2. Preparation

Position: Supine/30°, Arm abducted.
Landmarks: Triangle of Safety.
Rapid prep.

3. Procedure: Finger Thoracostomy

Incise: 4-6cm Transverse (Superior to rib).
Dissect: Forceps to pleura.
Puncture: Spread forceps.
Insert Finger: Expect rush of air/blood.
Sweep: 360° check (lung/adhesions).
Leave open in arrest.

4. Drain Insertion (If ROSC)

Insert large bore drain (28-32Fr).
Guide posterior & superior.
NEVER USE TROCAR
Connect seal. Secure.

10. Pericardiocentesis (Echo-Guided)

[Diagram: Apical Approach]

Indication: Cardiac Tamponade (Obstructive shock).

Equipment Required [Location: HALO Trolley / Pericardial Kit]

  • Echo Machine + Probe
  • Sterile cover & gel
  • 16-18G Teflon needle
  • J-wire & Dilator (6-8Fr)
  • Pigtail catheter (6-8Fr)
  • 3-way tap & 20ml Syringe
  • 1% Lidocaine
  • ECG Monitor

1. Call for Help

Consultant + NIC.
Cardiology / CT Surgery.
Alert Theatres.
Check Coagulation (unless arrest).

2. Preparation

Position: Semi-reclining (30-45°).
Monitor: Continuous ECG & BP.
Echo: Find largest pocket closest to skin.
Apical: Lat to apex beat (5th-7th ICS). Preferred.
Subcostal: Xiphisternum/Left costal margin.

3. Procedure

Prep: Sterile drape & LA.
Advance: Aspirate. Follow echo trajectory.
Fluid: Confirm non-clotting.
Confirm: Agitated saline bubbles on Echo (Time permitting).
Seldinger: Wire -> Dilate -> Pigtail.
Drain: Until haemodynamics improve.

4. Post-Procedure

Secure drain. Flush.
CXR: Check for Pneumothorax.
Monitor for Decompression Syndrome.
Avoid Aortic Dissection cases if possible.
22

11. eFONA (DAS Plan D)

[Diagram: Neck Anatomy]

Indication: Cannot Intubate, Cannot Oxygenate (CICO).

Equipment Required [Location: DIFFICULT AIRWAY TROLLEY Drawer 4]

  • Scalpel (Size 10)
  • Bougie (Coudé tip)
  • Tube (Size 6.0 cuffed)
  • Suction
  • 10mL Syringe
  • Tie/Tape

1. Declaration

Declare: "CICO. Transitioning to Plan D."
Help: Ensure help present.
Paralysis: Ensure FULL neuromuscular blockade.
Oxygen: Continue 100% via upper airway.

2. Position & Locate

Neck: Maximal Extension.
Operator: Stand on LEFT (if Right-handed).
Laryngeal Handshake: Non-dominant hand.
Identify: Cricothyroid Membrane (CTM).

3. Procedure: Scalpel Bougie Tube

Stabilise: Larynx with non-dominant hand.
Incision (Impalpable): 8-10cm Vertical Caudal-Cephalad. Dissect.
Incision (Palpable): Transverse Stab. Blade towards you.
Twist: Rotate blade 90° (Edge caudal).
Bougie: Insert bougie down blade into trachea.
Tube: Railroad Size 6.0 tube over bougie.
Ventilate: Inflate cuff. Connect circuit. Confirm CO2.

4. Failure?

Extend incision.
Smaller tube.
Change Operator.
24
